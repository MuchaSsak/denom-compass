## Acceptance as encoded
| Tradition | Councils | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Catholic | 1–21 | All binding |
| Eastern Orthodox | 1–7 | Some also count 879–880 and 1341–51; accept Trullo (692) canons |
| Oriental Orthodox | 1–3 | Reject Chalcedon (451) |
| Church of the East | 1–2 | Did not receive Ephesus; strongly rejects 553 (Theodore is their doctor) |
| Anglican, Lutheran, Reformed, Methodist, Baptist, Pentecostal, Non-denom | 1–4 | Doctrine affirmed under Scripture, not infallible. Anglicans formally the first four; many Lutherans/Reformed the Christology of 1–6; Anglo-Catholics all seven. Most Protestants reject Nicaea II on images (Anglican Art. 22). |
| Anabaptist | 1–2 | Trinitarian, but creeds carry no authority |
| Quaker, Churches of Christ, Adventist | none | No creeds/councils (Trinitarian content held from Scripture) |
| LDS, JW, Oneness | none | Reject Nicene/Constantinopolitan Trinity |
