# Polish glossary (EN → PL)

Binding terminology for every Polish string (UI catalog `src/locales/pl.po` and data overlays `src/data/pl/*.ts`). Prefer the term a Polish theologian of that tradition would use; when traditions differ, follow the tradition being described (e.g. Lutheran "Wieczerza Pańska", Catholic "Eucharystia").

## Style
- Informal singular "ty" to the user (quiz convention): „Czy wierzysz…”, „Twoje wyniki”.
- Short, plain, neutral. Never "powinien/powinno" for doctrinal claims (mirror EN's careful phrasing: "teaches", "holds").
- Polish quotes „…”, en dash – for ranges, decimal comma (1,42), "mln", "mld", "ok." for c./about, centuries "IV w.", "p.n.e./n.e." only when EN has BC/AD.
- Avoid declining denomination names inside templates; use a colon construction: „Najbliżej Ci do: {name}”.
- Bible references use Biblia Tysiąclecia sigla and comma: „Mt 28,19”, „J 1,1–14”, „2 Kor 13,13” (note: 2 Cor 13:14 EN = 2 Kor 13,13 in BT numbering), Rdz, Wj, Pwt, Iz, Ps, Mk, Łk, Dz, Rz, 1 Kor, Ga, Ef, Flp, Kol, 1 Tes, 1 Tm, Hbr, Jk, 1 P, 1 J, Ap.
