# Council details — the 21 councils the Catholic Church counts as ecumenical

Research dump compiled 2026-11-17 to feed a short "at a glance" detail page for each council. Main sources: the council introductions on Papal Encyclicals Online (papalencyclicals.net/councils/ecumNN.htm, which follow Tanner's *Decrees of the Ecumenical Councils*), the Catholic Encyclopedia "General Councils" article and single-council articles on New Advent, and Britannica (First Vatican Council). Wikipedia and encyclopedia.com were used only to cross-check. Attendance figures from ancient and medieval councils are unreliable. Where sources disagree the field gives a range or "about", and the disagreement is recorded on a "Contested:" line. The 1913 Catholic Encyclopedia often gives inflated medieval counts (for example, about 900 at Lateran I and about 1,000 at Lateran II), so the more cautious Tanner figures are preferred here. General reception: Catholics count all 21. The Eastern Orthodox count #1–7 and reject #8–21 as Latin synods; many Orthodox count the 879–880 council instead of #8. The Oriental Orthodox receive #1–3, and the Church of the East receives #1–2. Protestants generally accept the doctrine of #1–4, and some accept up to #6 or #7, without treating any council as infallible. The `dissent` arrays list only notable explicit rejections.
