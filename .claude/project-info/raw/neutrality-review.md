# Neutrality review (2026-11-17)

Immutable notes for the wording changes made after an objectivity/respect review of profiles, quiz text, councils and creeds (EN + PL). Each factual change with its source.

- Latter-day Saints naming: church style guide (2018) asks for the full name or "the Church of Jesus Christ"; avoid "Mormon"/"LDS" as names. https://newsroom.churchofjesuschrist.org/style-guide
- FLDS removed from LDS branches (disowned by the church); Community of Christ now Trinitarian (Community of Christ, "Basic Beliefs").
- Oneness Pentecostals: self-designation "Apostolic"; "Jesus Only" and "modalism" are outsiders' labels. UPCI "Our Beliefs" https://www.upci.org/about/our-beliefs
- Church of the East: Common Christological Declaration with the Catholic Church (1994) - legitimacy of "Theotokos" recognised; Church of the East calls Mary "Mother of Christ our God and Saviour". https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/pontifical_councils/chrstuni/documents/rc_pc_chrstuni_doc_11111994_assyrian-church_en.html
- Oriental Orthodox: Joint Commission with the Eastern Orthodox, agreed statements (Anba Bishoy 1989, Chambésy 1990); reception incomplete.
- Chalcedon: Church of the East separation (431) predates the Chalcedonian split (451), so Chalcedon is "one of the first lasting splits", not "the first".
- Nicene Creed usage: said at Sunday (not every) Catholic Mass (GIRM 68); many Lutheran services use the Apostles' Creed.
- Anglican origin: continuity claim from Augustine of Canterbury (597); Henry VIII (1534); Elizabethan Settlement (1559).
- Lutheran polity: ELCA has bishops (ELCA Constitution ch. 10); LCMS synod presidents.
- Quakers: FWCC "Finding Quakers Around the World" - most Friends are in programmed/evangelical meetings; Kenya has the largest national Quaker population, not a majority.
- Baptists: Second London Confession (1689) ch. 30 teaches spiritual presence in the Supper; Free Will Baptists reject eternal security (Treatise of the Faith and Practices).
- Anabaptists: "rebaptism" was the opponents' term; adult baptism on confession (Zürich, 21 January 1525).
- Families: "Historic peace churches" is the self-chosen umbrella of Mennonites/Brethren/Friends; Anglicans describe themselves as "Catholic and Reformed", so not filed as plain "Protestant".
