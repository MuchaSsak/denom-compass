# Marriage & abortion stances (added 2026-11-17)

Immutable source notes for the `marriage` and `abortion` rows in src/data/stances.ts. Scores follow official teaching; 0.5-0.75 where a tradition's main bodies disagree.

Same-sex marriage
- Catholic: CCC 2357-2359; DDF "Fiducia supplicans" (2023) allows spontaneous blessings of persons, not of unions; marriage remains man-woman.
- Orthodox / Oriental / Church of the East: marriage man-woman (e.g. Holy and Great Council, Crete 2016, "Sacrament of Marriage").
- Anglican: Lambeth 1998 Res. I.10 (majority view); Episcopal Church marries same-sex couples (2015); Church of England "Prayers of Love and Faith" blessings (2023); GAFCON provinces opposed.
- Lutheran: ELCA (2009 "bound conscience", local decision); Church of Sweden marries (2009); LCMS, WELS, most African Lutherans opposed.
- Reformed: PCUSA marries (2015); PCA, OPC, most global Reformed churches opposed.
- Methodist: UMC General Conference 2024 removed prohibitions (local decision); Global Methodist Church and many African conferences opposed.
- Baptist: SBC Baptist Faith & Message XVIII; small progressive bodies (Alliance of Baptists) affirm.
- Anabaptist: Mennonite Church USA rescinded membership guidelines (2022); conservative Mennonite, Amish, Mennonite Brethren opposed.
- Quaker: Britain Yearly Meeting celebrates same-sex marriages (2009); evangelical Friends (EFI, Kenya) opposed.
- Adventist, LDS, Jehovah's Witnesses, Pentecostal (AG), UPCI, Churches of Christ: man-woman only (official statements).

Abortion
- Catholic: CCC 2270-2275, Evangelium vitae 62; indirect loss under double effect permitted.
- Orthodox: grave sin; pastoral economia where the mother's life is at stake (e.g. Russian Orthodox "Bases of the Social Concept" XII.2).
- Anglican: Lambeth 1958/1998 strictly limited; Episcopal Church supports legal access (General Convention 2018, 2022).
- Lutheran: ELCA social statement on abortion (1991) - morally responsible in limited cases (life, rape/incest, fatal anomaly); LCMS only to save the mother's life.
- Reformed: PCUSA permits; PCA only to save the mother's life.
- Methodist: UMC Social Principles - "reluctant" acceptance in conflict of life with life; GMC stricter.
- Baptist, Pentecostal, non-denominational evangelical, Churches of Christ: generally only to save the mother's life.
- Adventist: "Statement on the Biblical View of Unborn Life" (2019) - exceptions incl. threats to life, rape/incest, severe anomalies.
- LDS: General Handbook 38.6.1 - exceptions for rape/incest, serious jeopardy to the mother's life/health, fatal defects.
- Jehovah's Witnesses: deliberate abortion is wrong; if the mother's life is at risk the couple decides.
- Quakers: no unified position; liberal and evangelical yearly meetings differ.
